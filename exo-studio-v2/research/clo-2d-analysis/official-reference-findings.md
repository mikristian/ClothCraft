# CLO 2D pattern workspace: official reference findings and EXO requirements

Research date: 2026-10-17. Scope: CLO 2D drafting, construction and inspection behaviors, using CLO Help Center articles and local `research/screenshots/clo-pattern-2334-reference.png`. These notes describe product behavior, not a claim that EXO already implements it. Some CLO Help Center pages describe different release generations; use behavior as the source of truth and verify exact menu labels against the target CLO release.

## 2. Selection, editing and view manipulation

Official CLO [Select](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012227067-Select) distinguishes **Transform Pattern** (click within a piece selects the complete pattern; hovered outline light blue; selected pattern yellow; lasso requires full enclosure) from **Edit Pattern** (click selects a point or segment; double click selects the whole pattern; Shift enables multiselection). [Select/Move Point/Segment](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000012828-Select-Move-Point-Segment) explicitly covers nodes/segments of patterns and internal shapes, overlapping-object pick menus, Shift-additive selection and selection marquees. [Transform Pattern](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012381568-Transform-Pattern) covers move, scale and rotate, with corner handles and pattern-level operations. [2D Pattern View Controls](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004270628-2D-Pattern-View-Controls) specifies F = zoom to selected pattern, empty-canvas context option = zoom extents all, and automatic smart panning during applicable drafting, sewing and topstitch operations. [Ruler & Guidelines](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010960074-Ruler-Guidelines) supports display units in inches/centimeters/millimeters, movable ruler origin and draggable or segment-aligned guidelines.

**EXO acceptance**: explicit transform-versus-geometry edit mode; distinguish piece, boundary segment, control point, internal line, notch, seam allowance, sewing pair and topstitch in hit testing. Hover feedback and selection handles must reflect the selected kind; support Shift multi-select, enclosure-only pattern marquee/lasso, exact drag and numeric transform, selection of overlapping objects, F to fit selection and fit all. Preserve independent 2D camera pan/zoom and visible grid and ruler unit settings. Include context actions near cursor, with undoable edits. At high zoom, node handles must remain perceptible; mouse/touch hit target may exceed rendered node radius.

## 3. Notches, edge geometry and graded positions

Official [Notch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015735467-Notch) and [Create Notch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013214287-Create-Notch) define attaching a notch to a pattern outline **or internal line**, showing a hover snap position and creating by click, or by right-click specifying exact segment location/split distances. [Select/Move Notch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013214367-Select-Move-Notch) describes selected highlighting, edge-constrained drag and precise distance entry via right click. [Edit Notch Properties](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015735687-Edit-Notch-Properties) lists name, type, angle (perpendicular or custom), corner-specific direction, flip and length. [Set Grading](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015798567-Set-Grading) identifies notch grading modes proportional to size or fixed distance.

**EXO acceptance**: model each notch by owner piece ID, stable edge/internal-line reference, normalized arc-distance or measured distance, style/type, length, angle, flip and optional grading rule. Render singly and as pairs if style requires; never bake red ticks into a background screenshot or independent absolute X/Y marks. Clicking and dragging projects movement back onto the host path and updates measured location. Edited vertices or curves recompute notch position and local tangent/normal. Split/delete edge operations must migrate/reject affected notch references explicitly, avoiding orphan marks. The side-seam pairs and upper-strip marks in the supplied image are representative visual regression fixtures.

## 4. Sewing, directional notches and paired segments

Official [Segment Sewing](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012381248-Segment-Sewing) connects two whole pattern segments (including pieces split by points), uses cursor movement to choose the *directional notch* on the second half, supports notch-based segmentation, and displays sewing-line length discrepancy in red when over 1 mm. [Free Sewing](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012226527-Free-Sewing) permits arbitrary start/end positions on outlines **or internal lines** and builds a pair of corresponding directed intervals. The older [2D Tool Segment Sewing](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000498767--2D-Tool-Segment-Sewing) warns crossed directional marks lead to reversed/twisted correspondence. [Edit Sewing](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012380148-Edit-Sewing) supports reversing direction, merge, deactivate/activate, hide/show and deleting the paired relationship.

**EXO acceptance**: store sewing as a relation between two **directed parametric edge intervals**, not a line drawn between two arbitrary pixels. Show source/target length and discrepancy warning; preview pairing and orientation while hovering, with arrows/notch glyphs. Offer Reverse Sewing and Remove Pair; free sewing can target interior construction lines. On geometry edit, recalculate pairing and length; remove pairs consistently in undo/history. Ensure visual notches created on physical cut edges remain separate from **direction indicators** attached to sewing relationships. This distinction is important: the reference image's red ticks alone do not prove its seam pairing orientation.

## 5. Seam allowances

Official [2D Seam Allowance](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015714428-2D-Seam-Allowance) permits per-segment addition to pattern outlines or internal lines and renders it light grey; selecting the host segment exposes the allowance. [Edit 2D Seam Allowance Properties](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015631887-Edit-2D-Seam-Allowance-Properties) exposes width, starting, ending and intersection join behavior. The newer [Edit Seam Allowance Properties](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013370408-Edit-Seam-Allowance-Properties) describes default allowances that follow pattern edits and an optional custom/manual editing mode that needs refresh after edits. [Show/Hide Seam Allowance](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013215787-Show-Hide-Seam-Allowance) covers independent visibility; CLO [2D Vertical Toggle Icons](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011148874-2D-Vertical-Toggle-Icons) places allowance, sewing, topstitch, baselines, grading and annotation visibility behind separate toggles.

**EXO acceptance**: seam allowance is computed offset geometry derived from selected segment(s), with editable units/width and corner join/end rules, optional custom mode, independent show/hide, consistent selection hit testing and deletion. Offset calculations must address concave corners, miters, self-intersections, short segments and curve offset approximation, and should not mutate the true cutting outline by default. Avoid assuming that the purple lines on the screenshot are seam allowances: grey is CLO's documented default allowance depiction.

## 6. Internal lines, construction markings, symbols and annotation

Official [Internal Polygon/Line](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012226687-Internal-Polygon-Line) creates interior polylines and closed shapes; successive clicks add points, double-click ends a line and clicking start closes a shape. Curves can be made with Ctrl or draggable Bezier handles; right-click allows exact length and position entry; Shift constrains to straight/45-degree runs; Delete/Backspace cancels a point and Esc/undo cancels drawing. [Internal Polygon/Line, older guide](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000481707-Internal-Polygon-Line) documents mirror creation and relative distances to pattern bounds. [Internal Line Variable Fold Angle](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/59498609129369-Internal-Line-Variable-Fold-Angle) (2026.1) describes uniform and end-tapered fold angles, plus symmetry-linked editing. [Pattern Instruction Lines](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015802448-Pattern-Instruction-Lines) supports attached construction symbols including Place on Fold. [Pattern Annotation](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000486687-Pattern-Annotation?page=1) creates editable text within patterns. [2D Vertical Toggle Icons](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011148874-2D-Vertical-Toggle-Icons) independently toggles baselines, reference lines, symmetric/instance lines and 2D sewing/topstitch.

**EXO acceptance**: represent boundary geometry and internal construction curves with separate typed objects and render layers. Support snap-to-existing vertex, centerline and orthogonal/45° constraints; line versus closed polygon modes; editable curve handles, exact lengths, mirrored construction, fold properties and symbols bound to a parent line. Keep persistent IDs and geometric coordinates for all internal marks. The reference image's three purple transverse lines, shoulder-adjacent purple lines, dashed center line, and tiny curved central symbol should be reconstructed as distinct selectable records after determining their intended semantics with a tool/Property Editor inspection; never flatten them into the garment fill.

## 7. Topstitch distinct from sewing

Official [Segment Topstitch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002224507--Tool-Segment-Topstitch) applies topstitch to one or multiple pattern/internal segments; [Free Topstitch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000656248-Free-Topstitch) spans arbitrary intervals and appears pink on selection. [Edit Topstitch](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002752908-Edit-Topstitch) exposes editable placement, style, type (texture/OBJ), offset, repeat/count and material/stitch specification. [Edit Topstitch, legacy](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000655808-Edit-Topstitch) explicitly supports moving endpoints, shortening and deleting a selected topstitch.

**EXO acceptance**: topstitch items are style-bearing decorations bound to edge/internal-line intervals with offset(s), stitch type/count and adjustable start/end, with their own toggle and Property Editor UI. They are not structural sewing relations and should never be used to simulate seamed connection. For screenshot parity, do not automatically treat purple lines as decorative stitching.

## 8. Measuring and production context

Official [Create POM](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025716774-Create-POM) creates single or grouped Points of Measure along outlines/internal lines, accepts grouped measurements, snapping and 45° guides, stores entries in a POM browser tab, and reports 3D length and grading-specific measurements. [Pattern Drafter](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/45053653355545-Pattern-Drafter) documents pattern drafting driven by measurement points and editable style measurement profiles. [Ruler & Guidelines](https://support.clo3d.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010960074-Ruler-Guidelines) confirms ruler units and guideline origin behaviors.

**EXO acceptance**: provide segment lengths, curve length and ruler overlays with selected measurement units; persistent POM paths with multiple vertices and accurate summed lengths; grouped measurement data; and precise placement/guides. Use model units for calculations and screen units only for drawing, ensuring changing 2D zoom cannot change physical measurement values. Include geometry validation after edits (closed piece, winding, self-intersection, degenerate edge length) and traceable undo/redo.

## 9. Proposed functional QA scenarios

1. Reproduce the screenshot's bodice outline and detached top strip with correctly fitted viewport; compare at native image aspect ratio and at 200% zoom; inspect curves, node markers, line categories, red ticks and grid.
2. In Edit Pattern, move neck and side-seam vertices; ensure attached notches, internal-line boundary anchors, POM measurements and seam allowance offsets track the edited geometry. Undo and redo restore all bound features atomically.
3. Add two notches by exact edge distances, edit angle/flip/length, move via drag; verify path-constrained positioning and correctness under curved-edge edits.
4. Join two nonmatching edges with Segment Sewing; show live length discrepancy and orientation arrows; reverse one side, then correct it. Repeat with free sewing across part of an internal line. Removing one sewing side must remove the pair.
5. Add seam allowance with mixed widths and concave corners, preview join types, toggle visibility, move a pattern control point, and confirm recalculated offset; custom/manual mode may preserve its independent shape as configured.
6. Draw interior polyline and closed polygon with numeric lengths, snapping and mirrored creation; cancel last point without discarding whole prior geometry; test Shift constraints and Bezier handle edits.
7. Add a topstitch with two offsets on an internal line, edit its start/end, toggle topstitch without hiding sewing, and inspect type/stitch count controls.
8. Select whole piece, segments, points, internals, notches and annotations separately; test overlapping-object selection, F-to-fit and extents fit, ruler unit switching and zoom-independent coordinate persistence.

## 10. Implementation sequencing

**P0 (critical geometry semantics)**: typed nodes/curves and distinct selection modes; accurate 2D camera transforms and hit testing; path-attached notches; boundary versus internal line identity; undo-safe editing and renderer overlays. The screenshot cannot be represented reliably without these.

**P1 (construction correctness)**: directed sewing interval pairs and mismatch indicators; seam allowance offset/join engine; measurement/POM model; topstitch interval objects; context-property editing and display toggles.

**P2 (advanced workflows)**: size-dependent notch grading, custom seam allowances, variable fold angles, mirrored linked editing, multi-style topstitches, production symbols and grouped POM reporting.

**Evidence caution**: Official CLO documentation establishes workflows and configurable properties. It does not establish that each colored stroke in the local crop represents a particular class of object. Confirm by selecting each stroke in native CLO and recording tool mode, selected entity name/type and Property Editor controls. Screenshot visual parity and semantic editing parity must be tested separately.
